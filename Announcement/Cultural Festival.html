About the Event

Join us for the Campus Cultural Festival, a fun-filled celebration bringing students and the campus community together.

Enjoy live music, cultural performances, student booths, traditional food, games, and activities throughout the day. Everyone is welcome to participate and experience the different cultures represented on campus.

Bring your friends and enjoy an afternoon of food, music, creativity, and community.
