About the club

Aerobic Club welcomes students of all fitness levels. Join group exercise sessions designed to build cardiovascular endurance, coordination, and confidence through movement.

What we do

Sessions include warm-ups, rhythmic aerobic routines, low-impact movement options, and cool-downs. Beginners are welcome.
